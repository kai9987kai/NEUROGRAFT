Controlled transition revision

Exploratory finite synthetic two-route assays with supplied state/action outcomes. The six arms share each ordered evidence stream and requested backup cap. Current dynamics are independently specified and withheld from the workbench; empirical-model planning error and current-dynamics error are separate measures. No neural policy, autonomous exploration, biological equivalence or checkpoint promotion is tested. Equal backup caps do not imply equal computation. Seeds draw geometry, dynamics and counts; cases and phases within a seed are correlated, not additional independent replicates.

Current dynamics and empirical-model consistency are different evidence. A low Bellman residual can describe a perfectly solved stale model. Prediction scores are excess distribution Brier risk; start regret evaluates a frozen deterministic policy analytically, without feedback. These are not sampled goal-reaching rates.

Cumulative retains baseline counts plus every new outcome. Recent retains baseline counts for untouched state/actions; the first new outcome replaces that pair with its last W explicitly supplied new outcomes. Baseline aggregate counts have no reconstructed chronology.

Conditional adverse-run stress test: after the declared unbiased stationary sample, supply W collisions at each target action without changing true dynamics. This conditions on an adverse run and is not an estimate of its frequency or unconditional expected performance.

All declared cases and arms are retained. Means weight seeds equally; there are no significance tests, tuning, selection or promotion gates. The fixed default eight seeds are a local descriptive pilot.
