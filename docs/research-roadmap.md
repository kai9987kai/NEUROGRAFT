# Evidence-led research priorities after v0.15

31 October 2026. These are proposed next experiments, not implemented features or established benefits.

| Priority | Owning evidence | Next controlled experiment | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Reduce avoidable neural action failures | v10 learned residual navigation still has frequent collisions; v11 prediction loss does not improve raw success | Set-valued shortest-action imitation and an observed-wall feasibility penalty in a matched 2x2 objective study. Hold architecture, initialization, sample identities and update budgets fixed; evaluate the raw unmasked policy, all topologies, new declared seeds | Full-map teacher action sets remain privileged training labels. Better label ties cannot reveal unobserved walls. Success, collisions and SPL must improve behaviorally; prediction loss alone is insufficient |
| 2. Diagnose longer learned memory | `GraphCircuit.forward` initializes state per supplied sequence; live inference reconstructs a short sliding window. Saved recurrent state is not used as persistent policy context | First build history-alias diagnostic pairs where an earlier observed wall distinguishes identical recent suffixes. Then compare windowed versus opt-in episodic state, with erased/shuffled-history controls, burn-in and matched sequence budgets | Additional state needs reset/weight-update/checkpoint semantics and compute accounting. It must not receive hidden geometry or inherit a symbolic planner's score as a neural result |
| 3. Learn when to revisit avoided actions | v15 adaptive evidence remains wrong without fresh visits; two visits do not satisfy its guard. Live visual gates are overlays rather than changing collision physics | Separate physical-changing-world trajectory assay with matched interaction budgets: passive cumulative, passive recent/adaptive, fixed periodic revisits and uncertainty-guided revisits | Supplied per-action outcomes are not autonomous exploration. Reopening cannot be inferred from avoidance; policy labels computed by the live teacher must not leak into a claimed teacher-free assay |
| 4. Compare evidence rules without outcome-driven tuning | v15 adaptive selection trades stationary retention against delayed adaptation; guarded recent often explains much of its behavior | Declare disjoint development/confirmation seeds before comparing the fixed rule with adaptive windows or run-length inference. Preserve matched-prefix ambiguity and conditional adverse controls; record all false switches and delays | No causal method distinguishes different hidden truths with identical observed prefixes. Predictor improvement does not guarantee lower decision regret |
