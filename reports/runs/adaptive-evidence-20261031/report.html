Exploratory finite synthetic supplied-outcome streams, not autonomous exploration, neural learning, biological equivalence or checkpoint promotion. Interpretation and reproducibility

Score Beta(.5,.5) binary move predictions before updates; local evidence = .95 * evidence + log(p_recent(outcome)/p_cumulative(outcome)). Select recent iff fresh_count >= 4 and evidence > log(4), otherwise cumulative. Guarded recent forecasts cumulative until four previous fresh events, then recent. Planning uses unsmoothed positive integer counts; no edges are fabricated.

W forced collisions per target under unchanged truth, followed by 48 unconditional recovery samples. Conditional stress, not its frequency.

Identical baselines and W collisions per target are presented to two memories with different evaluation-only truths. Their forecasts, selected graphs and diagnostic state must be identical.

Retain every case, stage and seed. Equal-weight seed means and paired seed differences are descriptive; stages/cases within a seed are correlated, not independent replicates. No significance test, tuning, dominance gate or promotion.

Complete ordered outcomes, forecasts, switches, selection delays, seed differences, model states, values and truth are in report.json. Plain verification rederives causal forecast scores and independent metrics. Strict replay also regenerates seeded streams and planning. No checkpoint is loaded or promoted.
